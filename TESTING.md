# Testing

## Official homologation route

The official hill test follows the steep road leaving Mogilevka in Chernarus.

The road sign near the hardest part of the ascent is the primary reference
point. A vehicle must continue beyond this point without losing all forward
movement and rolling backwards unexpectedly.

## Test checklist

For each supported vehicle:

1. Start with a complete and undamaged vehicle.
2. Approach the climb using normal acceleration.
3. Record the gear and speed near the sign.
4. Confirm that the vehicle completes the ascent.
5. Test low and medium RPM recovery.
6. Test reverse behavior.
7. Test maximum speed on a suitable straight road.
8. Listen for abnormal engine audio.
9. Check steering and stability.
10. Restart the server and confirm the vehicle remains available.

## Approved release observations

- M3S: controlled ascent with substantially improved hill performance.
- Ada 4x4: strong and controllable ascent.
- Olga 24: completes the route without its original severe power loss.
- Sarka 120: completes the route, while retaining its lighter handling.
- Gunter 2: improved second- and third-gear response and successful ascent.
- Humvee: automatic ascent approved with smoother clutch coupling.

The goal is useful and enjoyable driving, not identical performance across
all vehicles.
